# COVAUNT launch film — treatment

## Working title

**COVAUNT — The Vote Was Unanimous. The Payment Was Denied.**

## Editorial direction

An 84-second proof film with the feeling of a civic ledger being opened at night: parchment ground, dark ink, oxblood signals, brass rules, and a single consent line that travels through every scene. The camera never floats without purpose. It either tracks a ledger row, follows an allocation line, or cuts on a verified receipt.

The film is score-led rather than voice-led. Captions carry the argument in short, readable sentences. Music is an original locally synthesized score generated by huashu-motion from the film's scene structure; impact cues land on the allocation, approval, rejection, callback, and final wordmark. No stock imagery, fictional dashboards, or invented settlement states are used.

## Three considered directions

1. **Ledger / chosen direction.** A continuous copper consent spine moves through a sequence of real-evidence cards. This makes the core differentiator legible in the first five seconds and gives the many proof points one spatial grammar.
2. **Assembly hall.** A more literal editorial room with council columns and donor seals. Strong for governance, but less efficient at showing the callback distinction inside 84 seconds.
3. **Receipt atlas.** A fast, documentary montage of journal evidence and explorer references. Strong on proof, but less memorable as a product film.

Direction 1 is used because it keeps the product idea, not the interface, as the hero while still showing the live production routes and actual transaction records.

## Visual rules

- 1920×1080 composition, 30fps or higher.
- Background: `#efece4`; ink: `#202924`; signal: `#9d352e`; rule/brass: `#b28a58`; success: `#3d6b4d`.
- Georgia-style editorial headlines, Arial body copy, Cascadia Mono evidence labels where available.
- Every state is labelled as one of `DAO APPROVED`, `VAULT ACCEPTED`, `VAULT REJECTED`, `SETTLED`, or `CALLBACK SETTLED`.
- A DAO vote is never animated as a payment. The downstream callback is a distinct visual event.
- The 9 NEAR and 2 NEAR proof cards use the real transaction references recorded in `COVAUNT_VIDEO_EVIDENCE.md`.
- The Work & Trust segment uses only the three live task records and the verified Work account state.

## Internal review passes

1. **Story pass:** verify that the hook states the security achievement before any product explanation; remove any line that could imply a vote equals settlement.
2. **Craft pass:** review representative frames at full resolution for clipped type, collision between evidence labels and the consent spine, and audio landings around the denial beat. Run `om check`, `om still`, `om sound`, `om render`, and `om qa --sheet`.

## Delivery decision

The film uses no narration because the factual story is dense and the editorial captions remain readable at reduced size. A separate short screen-recorded proof can be used as a fallback if the premium render is not uploaded before the hard submission gate.
